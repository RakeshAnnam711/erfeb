Live-Selling and CSC Handoff Cart Behavior - Implementation Summary
===================================================================

1. Requirement
------------------
Live-selling product experience:

- Dedicated live-selling PLP/category. Products visible on PLP, but not
  directly purchasable by the customer.
- Hide price and Add to Cart/quick add on PLP and PDP (also hide quantity
  selector and sticky Add to Cart on PDP).
- Show a live-selling badge and item ID.
- After order placement, store live-selling host name and event date on the
  order/order line item.

CSC / BM handoff cart behavior:

- Products added by Customer Service through Business Manager and handed off
  to the customer must be locked - customer cannot remove, change quantity,
  or wishlist them, and the promo/coupon box is hidden.
- Products the customer adds themselves from the storefront must stay fully
  editable, even in the same (mixed) basket.
- Remove All clears the whole basket, CSC and storefront items alike.


2. What Was Built - Live-Selling PLP/PDP
--------------------------------------------
- Custom attributes added (see section 5 for the full list) so Business
  Manager can flag a product/category as live-selling and store badge/item
  ID/host/event date.
- A live-selling decorator (models/product/decorators/liveSelling.js) exposes
  those fields on the product model for PLP/PDP templates.
- PLP/PDP templates hide price and Add to Cart when a product is
  live-selling, and show the badge + item ID instead.
- Checkout logic (doPrePlaceOrder.js) copies live-selling data onto the
  order and order line item once payment succeeds.


3. What Was Built - CSC Handoff Cart Locking
-------------------------------------------------
- ProductLineItem flags isCSCHandoffLineItem / isStorefrontLineItem
  distinguish CSC-added items (locked) from customer-added items (editable),
  supporting mixed baskets.
- agentBasketLineItemLocks.js is the central helper: classifies line items,
  tracks locked UUIDs, and decorates the cart model so templates know what to
  hide.
- Cart controller guards block Remove/Update Quantity/Coupon changes on
  locked line items.
- Cart, mini cart, and checkout templates hide Remove/Wishlist/quantity
  controls and the promo box for locked items; storefront items keep them.
- Remove All clears the entire basket (added to cart page and mini cart).


4. Refinements After Initial Build
---------------------------------------
4.1 CSC Product Detail custom fields (agent-facing, BM config only)

Business Manager's Customer Service Center Settings > Product Detail area
now shows an "Is Live Selling Line Item" checkbox and "Live Selling Host
Name" text field when an agent adds a product to a basket. These bind to
ProductLineItem attributes that already existed - no new attributes were
created.

4.2 Lock only live-selling line items, not every CSC-added item

Originally any CSC-added product was locked regardless of live-selling
status. Changed so only live-selling items lock; a plain CSC-added product
now behaves like a normal customer item. isLineItemLiveSelling() resolves
this the same way at both cart-lock time and checkout: the agent's explicit
checkbox choice (checked or unchecked) wins if they touched it, otherwise it
falls back to the catalog product's own isLiveSellingProduct flag. Locks
also now release automatically if an agent unchecks the box after initially
checking it - previously a lock, once set, could never be cleared.

4.3 Session lock-cache staleness fix

The session-cached list of "locked" line item UUIDs used to only grow, never
shrink, so a line item briefly mis-locked during its own add-to-cart request
could stay locked forever even after its real attribute was corrected. Fixed
by purging the session cache to match the real attribute state on every
check.

4.4 isCSCHandoffOrder (order-level)

New order attribute, set independently of isLiveSellingOrder, true whenever
any line item on the order was CSC-locked. Lets a plain CSC handoff order be
told apart from a live-selling CSC handoff order in reporting.

4.5 5-minute CSC line item auto-expiration

A CSC-locked line item still sitting unpurchased 5 minutes after being added
is automatically removed from the basket. Checked reactively on cart
page-load/navigation, plus a client-side poll every 30 seconds while the
cart page is open (server always re-validates elapsed time; the client only
triggers the check). Known limit: this is not a true background timer - SFCC
has no supported way for a scheduled job to reach into an arbitrary
customer's basket, so if the customer closes the tab and never returns nothing
removes the item until their next visit.

4.6 PLP live-selling badge position

Moved from the tile body (below the image, in normal text flow) to overlay
the top-left corner of the product image, matching the site's existing
promotional badge pattern. Item ID text stays in its original spot near the
product name.

- autobahn/data/meta/rvw_som_integration/meta/system-objecttype-extensions.xml

Custom attributes to confirm exist in BM after import:

- Category: isLiveSellingCategory
- Product: isLiveSellingProduct, liveSellingItemID, liveSellingBadgeText,
  liveSellingHostName, liveSellingEventDate
- Order: isLiveSellingOrder, isCSCHandoffOrder, liveSellingHostName,
  liveSellingEventDate
- ProductLineItem: isLiveSellingLineItem, liveSellingItemID,
  liveSellingHostName, liveSellingEventDate, isCSCHandoffLineItem,
  isStorefrontLineItem

No SFCC custom object types are used anywhere in this work - only system
object custom attributes on existing objects.

Product/category setup for the live-selling PLP to show a product: product
must be online, searchable, assigned to the live-selling category, and
available/in stock; rebuild the product index and clear cache after changes.
Dev category used: live-selling-dev-products (production category can be a
different ID - the code is attribute-driven, not tied to this specific ID).


6. Testing Steps
---------------------
Live-selling PLP/PDP: import metadata, assign product to live-selling
category, mark online/searchable/in-stock, fill live-selling fields, rebuild
index, open the category PLP and confirm price/Add to Cart are hidden and
badge/item ID show; open PDP and confirm price/quantity/Add to
Cart/sticky-add are all hidden.

CSC handoff cart: add a product via CSC, hand off the basket, log in as the
customer, confirm the CSC item has no Remove/Wishlist/quantity-update and
the promo box is hidden; add a product manually as the customer and confirm
it has all controls; click Remove All and confirm the cart empties
completely.

Order attributes: place an order containing a live-selling/CSC product,
check isCSCHandoffOrder / isLiveSellingOrder / liveSellingHostName /
liveSellingEventDate on the order, and the matching fields plus
isCSCHandoffLineItem on the order line item.

8. Open Topic - Live-Selling Pricing, Auto Item IDs, Visibility, Category Activation
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

8.1 Event pricing (separate price book for live-selling)

Asking for: a special price during the live-selling event, visible ONLY on
the live-selling page, that reverts to normal automatically afterward, and
can be bulk-updated instead of edited product by product.

What's possible:
- A dedicated live-selling price book, bulk-updatable via Business Manager's
  native price import (spreadsheet-style bulk tool available, no custom
  tooling needed for that part).
- The simple way to activate a price book (assign it to the site) affects
  the price EVERYWHERE that product is shown, not just the live-selling
  page - this does not meet the "nowhere else" requirement.
- To truly scope the price to only the live-selling page, the price must be
  looked up in code specifically for that page, with the special price book
  never assigned to the site by default. This also means the normal price
  restores automatically after the event with no extra step, since it was
  never actually replaced anywhere else.

Questions to ask the business:
- What should happen if a product has no price set in the live-selling price
  book when the event goes live - show it anyway (wrong price), hide it, or
  flag it for review?
- Should the event go live on a pre-set schedule (specific start/end dates),
  or via a manual on/off switch someone flips?

8.2 Automatic item ID numbering

Asking for: item IDs auto-numbered 1 through N based on how many products
are in the live-selling category, in an order the business controls, with no
manual entry per product.

What's possible:
- Business Manager already has a native "set product order within a
  category" feature (drag-and-drop or type a position) - this covers "order
  the business defines" with no new tooling.
- Numbering itself can either be calculated fresh every time (simple, but
  numbers can shift if a product is removed from the middle of the list) or
  assigned once and remembered permanently (numbers stay stable, needs a
  small automated job to assign new numbers as products are added).
- The permanent/stable option can run on SFCC's built-in Job Scheduler
  (Business Manager > Administration > Operations > Jobs) - the platform's
  equivalent of a cron job. A custom job step, scheduled on a recurring
  interval, would scan the live-selling category in the business-defined
  sort order and assign the next sequence number to any product that
  doesn't have one yet. This repo already has a real example of this exact
  pattern to follow: OrderExportForSOM (rvw_som_integration/jobs.xml,
  currently disabled) is a custom job step wired to a plain JS script. The
  exact minimum scheduling interval Business Manager allows was not
  confirmed - worth checking directly in the Job Scheduler UI, especially if
  numbering needs to update quickly during an active event.

Questions to ask the business:
- Should item numbers stay permanent once assigned, or is it fine for them
  to shift if an earlier product is removed?

8.3 Hide live-selling products from search, other categories, and normal
navigation

Asking for: a live-selling product should only be findable through the
live-selling page/URL - not search, not other listing pages, not other
categories.

Important conflict: today's live-selling page is built on the same
"searchable" switch that controls whether a product shows up in search and
regular category pages. Turning that switch off to satisfy this requirement
would also break the live-selling page itself, since it currently depends on
the same switch to show anything at all. Making this work cleanly requires
rebuilding the live-selling page to fetch its products directly, independent
of search - a real piece of additional work, not a small setting change.

Questions to ask the business:
- Confirm this is worth the additional rebuild work described above, given
  it is a bigger change than a settings tweak.

8.4 Category activation on/off

Asking for: a single on/off switch for the live-selling event - when on,
products show on the live-selling page only; when off, they disappear from
there and behave normally (if applicable).

What's possible: the on/off switch and scheduling are already native,
solved SFCC features (categories have their own active/inactive toggle and
go-live/expire dates) - nothing new needed for the switch itself.

Question to ask the business (currently undefined, per their own
requirement wording):
- What should happen to a product that is assigned to BOTH a normal category
  AND the live-selling category at the same time? Should it be locked down
  (no price/Add to Cart) everywhere it appears while the event is active,
  even in its normal category - or fully purchasable normally, and only
  restricted specifically on the live-selling page? Today's code would
  default to the first option by accident of how the flag check works, not
  by deliberate decision.
