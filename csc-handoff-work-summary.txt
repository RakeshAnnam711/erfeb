Live-Selling and CSC Handoff Cart Behavior - Implementation Summary
===================================================================
Requirement
--------------
1. Live-selling product experience

The business wants a dedicated live-selling product listing/category where
products are visible to customers, but customers cannot buy them directly from
the storefront.

Expected behavior:

- Create/configure a dedicated live-selling PLP/category.
- Live-selling products should display on PLP.
- Hide product price on PLP.
- Hide Add to Cart / quick add on PLP.
- Hide product price on PDP.
- Hide Add to Cart, quantity selector, and sticky Add to Cart on PDP.
- Show a live-selling badge.
- Show a live-selling item ID.
- After successful order placement, send/store live-selling host name and event
  date on the order/order line item.

2. CSC / BM handoff cart behavior

Products are added by Customer Service from Business Manager / CSC and handed
off to the customer. Those CSC-added products must not be editable by the
customer.

Expected behavior:

- CSC-added products must be locked in the cart.
- Customer should not remove CSC-added products.
- Customer should not update CSC product quantity.
- Customer should not move CSC products to wishlist.
- Promo/coupon box should be hidden for CSC handoff baskets.
- Customer can still continue shopping normally.
- Products added by customer from storefront should remain editable.
- Storefront-added products should allow remove, wishlist, and quantity update.
- Remove All should clear the full basket.


2. How We Achieved Live-Selling PLP/PDP
--------------------------------------
Step 1: Added metadata fields

We added custom attributes through system-objecttype-extensions.xml so Business
Manager can store live-selling data.

Category:

- isLiveSellingCategory

Product:

- isLiveSellingProduct
- liveSellingItemID
- liveSellingBadgeText
- liveSellingHostName
- liveSellingEventDate

Order:

- liveSellingHostName
- liveSellingEventDate

ProductLineItem:

- liveSellingItemID
- liveSellingHostName
- liveSellingEventDate

Step 2: Added product model decoration

Created a live-selling decorator so PLP/PDP product models can expose:

- isLiveSellingProduct
- liveSellingItemID
- liveSellingBadgeText
- liveSellingHostName
- liveSellingEventDate

This allows templates to decide whether to show normal purchase controls or
live-selling-only display.

Step 3: Updated PLP templates

On product tiles, when a product is live-selling:

- Price is hidden.
- Add to Cart / quick add is hidden.
- Badge is displayed.
- Item ID is displayed.

Step 4: Updated PDP templates

On PDP, when a product is live-selling:

- Price is hidden.
- Quantity selector is hidden.
- Add to Cart button is hidden.
- Sticky Add to Cart is hidden.

Step 5: Updated order placement logic

During pre-place-order logic, live-selling product data is copied from product
or basket line item context to order/order line item custom attributes.

This is used for downstream reporting/export after payment is successful.


3. How We Achieved CSC Handoff Cart Locking
-------------------------------------------
Step 1: Added line-item flags

ProductLineItem custom attributes were added:

- isCSCHandoffLineItem
- isStorefrontLineItem

Purpose:

- isCSCHandoffLineItem means the item came from CSC/BM handoff and should be
  locked.
- isStorefrontLineItem means the customer added the item from the storefront
  and should still be able to edit/remove it.

Step 2: Added helper for lock detection

Added agentBasketLineItemLocks.js.

This helper is responsible for:

- Tracking locked CSC line item UUIDs.
- Reading persisted ProductLineItem custom attributes.
- Decorating cart/shipping models with isAgentLockedLineItem.
- Identifying storefront-added line items.
- Helping controller/template code decide which controls to hide.

Step 3: Updated cart controller guards

Cart controller behavior was updated so locked CSC line items cannot be changed
by normal storefront actions.

Blocked for CSC-locked line items:

- Remove product.
- Update quantity.
- Coupon add/remove where basket is restricted.

Allowed:

- Remove All clears the complete cart.
- Storefront-added products remain editable.

Step 4: Updated templates

Cart, mini cart, and checkout/cart product card templates were updated to hide
restricted controls when a line item is marked as locked.

For CSC-locked products:

- Hide remove icon/button.
- Hide Add to Wishlist.
- Hide quantity update controls.
- Hide promo/coupon section for restricted handoff basket.

For storefront-added products:

- Show remove.
- Show wishlist.
- Show quantity update.

Step 5: Added Remove All

Remove All was added to:

- Cart page.
- Mini cart.

Remove All is intended to clear the whole basket, including both CSC and
storefront products.

Note:
Remove All should not be shown on checkout page based on the later requirement.

For products to appear on the live-selling PLP:

- Product must be assigned to the live-selling category.
- Product must be online.
- Product must be searchable.
- Product must be available/in stock.
- Product index must be rebuilt after changes.
- Cache may need to be cleared.

The final production category can be created later by the business/team. The
code is attribute-driven, so the category ID can change as long as products and
attributes are configured correctly.


5. Custom Objects / Attributes Used For This Task
-------------------------------------------------
No SFCC CustomObjectMgr custom object type is used for this live-selling/CSC
handoff implementation.

For this ticket, we used system object custom attributes only. These are fields
added to existing SFCC system objects such as Category, Product, Order, and
ProductLineItem.

System object attributes used:

Category:

- isLiveSellingCategory

Product:

- isLiveSellingProduct
- liveSellingItemID
- liveSellingBadgeText
- liveSellingHostName
- liveSellingEventDate

Order:

- liveSellingHostName
- liveSellingEventDate

ProductLineItem:

- liveSellingItemID
- liveSellingHostName
- liveSellingEventDate
- isCSCHandoffLineItem
- isStorefrontLineItem

Why we used these attributes:

- Product/category attributes control whether a product behaves as
  live-selling on PLP/PDP.
- Product live-selling fields provide badge, item ID, host name, and event date.
- Order and ProductLineItem fields store live-selling details after order
  placement.
- isCSCHandoffLineItem locks CSC/BM handoff products in the customer cart.
- isStorefrontLineItem keeps customer-added products editable in mixed baskets.

Files where these attributes are defined:

- autobahn/data/clientmeta/autobahn_client_core/meta/system-objecttype-extensions.xml
- autobahn/data/meta/rvw_som_integration/meta/system-objecttype-extensions.xml

Important:
Do not import or maintain unrelated custom-objecttype-definitions.xml entries
for this requirement. They are not part of this task.


6. Testing Steps
----------------
Live-selling PLP/PDP test:

1. Import metadata.
2. Assign product to live-selling category.
3. Mark product online.
4. Mark product searchable.
5. Ensure product has inventory/availability.
6. Fill live-selling product fields:
   - isLiveSellingProduct
   - liveSellingItemID
   - liveSellingBadgeText
   - liveSellingHostName
   - liveSellingEventDate
7. Rebuild product index.
8. Clear cache if needed.
9. Open live-selling category PLP.
10. Confirm product appears.
11. Confirm price and Add to Cart are hidden.
12. Confirm badge and item ID are visible.
13. Open PDP.
14. Confirm price, quantity, Add to Cart, and sticky Add to Cart are hidden.

CSC handoff cart test:

1. Login to BM/CSC.
2. Create basket/order for customer.
3. Add product from CSC.
4. Handoff basket to customer.
5. Login as customer on storefront.
6. Open mini cart and cart page.
7. Confirm CSC product is visible.
8. Confirm CSC product does not show:
   - Remove
   - Add to Wishlist
   - Quantity update
9. Confirm promo/coupon box is hidden.
10. Add a new product manually from storefront.
11. Confirm storefront product does show:
   - Remove
   - Add to Wishlist
   - Quantity update
12. Click Remove All.
13. Confirm full cart becomes empty.

Order attribute test:

1. Place order with live-selling/CSC product.
2. Open order in BM.
3. Check order custom attributes:
   - liveSellingHostName
   - liveSellingEventDate
4. Check order product line item custom attributes:
   - liveSellingItemID
   - liveSellingHostName
   - liveSellingEventDate
   - isCSCHandoffLineItem, if applicable
