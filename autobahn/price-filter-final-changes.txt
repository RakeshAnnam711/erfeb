Final fix for PLP highest-price product removal after applying price filter:

Root cause:
- The server-side price max value (`pmax`) was being converted and passed directly into `productSearch.setPriceMax(...)`.
- After currency conversion / float rounding, the upper bound could become slightly lower than the actual highest product price.
- Because of that, the highest-priced product was getting excluded when the price filter was applied.

Final file changed:
autobahn_client_core/cartridges/autobahn_client_core/cartridge/scripts/search/search.js

Final change made:
- Adjusted server-side `pmax` handling to add a small buffer before calling `productSearch.setPriceMax(...)`.
- This keeps the upper price bound inclusive after conversion and rounding.

Why this fixes the issue:
- The highest-priced product is no longer dropped from the result set when `pmax` matches the top product price.
